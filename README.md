# GA4 Optimizer

**Make Google Analytics usable again.**

GA4 Optimizer is a **100% free, private Chrome extension** that fixes the most frustrating parts of the Google Analytics 4 interface. Built by a Senior Data Analyst who got tired of the clunky GA4 UI. Your GA4 data never leaves your browser.

---

## Top Features That Will Save You Hours

- **Universal Data Dictionary**
  Stop asking "what does this event mean?" in Slack. Bring your company's tracking plan directly into GA4. See custom definitions for events, dimensions, and metrics as hover tooltips right inside your reports. Syncs automatically with Google Sheets.

- **Advanced Table Filters**
  Miss the advanced table filters from Universal Analytics? We brought them back for Standard Reports. Combine multiple conditions (e.g., Views > 10k AND Path contains /shop) to slice your data with precision.

- **Bulk Annotations**
  Stop repeating yourself 50 times. Create a single annotation and push it across dozens of GA4 properties and 360 subproperties simultaneously.

- **Bring Back Exit Pages**
  GA4 removed the standard Exit Pages report. We brought it back. Generate a pre-configured Exit Pages report template with a single click.

- **Fix Shared Links ("No Access" Error)**
  Tired of getting "Missing Permissions" when a colleague shares a GA4 link? This usually happens when multiple Google accounts are in use. GA4 Optimizer automatically detects the issue and helps you retry with other logged-in accounts.

- **Read-only Exploration Date Changer**
  When someone shares an Exploration report, you can finally change the date yourself without having to copy the whole report.

- **1-Click Date Comparisons & Presets**
  Stop fighting the calendar. Compare MoM or YoY instantly, including a highly accurate "matching day of week" alignment. Plus, quick presets like Last 7 Days or Last Month.

## Reporting & Analysis Enhancements

- **Percentage Change Highlighter**
  Make key trends pop. Visually identify significant positive or negative metric changes when comparing dates using custom color-coded thresholds.

- **On-the-Fly Calculated Metrics**
  Create and save custom rates (like Exit Rate or Conversion Rate) directly within Standard Reports and Explorations.

- **Automatic Statistical Significance**
  Instantly know if your A/B test results are trustworthy with built-in confidence calculations right inside your reports.

- **Delta % Share Change**
  Go beyond raw percentages. See the change in a metric's contribution to the total to get a much clearer picture of performance shifts.

- **Sticky Report Headers**
  Never lose track of which metric column you're viewing when scrolling through long Standard Reports.

---

## Exploration Power Tools (Hover & Discover)

- **Annotations in Explorations**
  Stop guessing what caused a data spike. We surface your property annotations directly inside your Exploration charts and tables so you always have immediate context for anomalies.

- **Maximize View**
  Get your screen real estate back. Instantly expand Freeform Explorations to the maximum columns/rows possible, with intelligent pagination controls and keyboard shortcuts.

- **Percentage of Column Total**
  Hover over any cell to quickly see a row's contribution to the column total. No clicking required.

- **Performance vs. "All Users"**
  Quickly assess any segment's performance relative to the All Users baseline on hover.

- **Collapsible Panels**
  Maximize your analysis workspace with a quick toggle to collapse the Variables and Tab Settings panels.

- **Detailed Results Shortcut**
  Force Explorations to use "More detailed results" (less sampling) via a quick keyboard shortcut.

## Workflow & UI Upgrades

- **Team Sync & Backup**
  Stop rebuilding the same setups on every computer. Share your Data Dictionary, custom Calculated Metrics, highlight rules, and annotation colors with your entire team using a simple share code, file, or hosted URL.

- **Advanced Click-to-Copy**
  - Single-click: copy cell values
  - Double-click: copy full text content
  - Triple-click (Explorations): copy rich hover-tooltip data

- **Sticky Quick Calculator**
  A handy, draggable calculator widget is always accessible for quick rate and uplift calculations on any page.

- **Highlight Sampling Icon**
  A subtle pulsing animation draws your attention to the data quality icon if your report is secretly using sampled data.

- **Line Graph Customizations**
  Customize your line chart colors to match your reporting and presentation needs.

- **Custom Definitions Copy/Paste**
  Setting up a new property? Migrate custom dimensions, custom metrics, and calculated metrics between GA4 properties. Perfect for agencies and power users.

- **500 Rows Visibility**
  Unlock the ability to view up to 500 rows per page in Standard and Advertising Reports.

- **International Formatting**
  Full support for international users that use comma number decimal formatting (e.g., 1.234,56).
