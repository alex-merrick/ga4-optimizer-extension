# Copy & UI Text Style Rules

These rules apply to ALL visible text on the website: buttons, labels, tooltips, CTAs, headings, descriptions, and any user-facing copy.

## Avoid AI-Generated Text Giveaways

- **No em dashes (—):** Use commas, colons, periods, or slashes instead. Em dashes are a dead giveaway for AI-written text.
- **No semicolons in marketing copy:** Keep sentences short and punchy.
- **Banned words/phrases:** delve, unleash, supercharge, landscape, testament, crucial, streamline, leverage (as verb), dive into, elevate, empower, harness, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, seamlessly, effortlessly, at its core, it's worth noting.
- **No filler openers:** Don't start sentences with "In today's...", "In the world of...", "When it comes to...", "It goes without saying..."
- **No excessive hedging:** Drop "arguably," "essentially," "fundamentally," "interestingly," "notably."
- **No emojis** in UI text, headings, or meta data.

## Tone

- Write like a human who actually uses GA4 daily.
- Be direct. Short sentences. Active voice.
- If a phrase sounds like it could be in a LinkedIn post, rewrite it.
- Prefer informal separators: use `//` or `-` or `:` over `—` when joining a heading with a subclause.
